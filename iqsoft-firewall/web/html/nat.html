Port forwarding

Send traffic that arrives on the WAN side at a port, or a range of ports, to a host on your LAN. The firewall opens the forwarded ports for you. Changes are saved straight away and take effect after you press Apply.

Name Protocol External port Forwarded to On

1:1 NAT

Map a public address on the WAN side to one host on your LAN, in both directions. This only translates addresses. To let traffic in, also add a FORWARD rule whose destination is the internal host. The external address must already be configured on the WAN interface.

Name External IP Internal IP On
