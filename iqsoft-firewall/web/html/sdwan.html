SD-WAN

SD-WAN uses several internet connections at once. It checks each one every few seconds, sends traffic over the ones that work, and moves traffic away from a connection the moment it stops answering.

Turn on SD-WAN Needs at least one enabled WAN link.
Changes to links, rules and this switch are only saved until you press Commit.
